ground_detail.jpg: ground finer than the 25 cm HiRISE photo, used within about 60 m of the walker.

Source: NASA InSight, Instrument Deployment Camera (IDC) workspace orthomosaic, sol 12, 2 mm per pixel, colour,
D_LRGBI0012_CPG010060ORRAS__F2MMWKSM1.VIC, PDS Imaging Node (planetarydata.jpl.nasa.gov/img/data/nsyt/insight_cameras/
data/sol/0012/mipl/rdr/mosaic/). Credit NASA/JPL-Caltech. Location: Homestead hollow, Elysium Planitia, not Candor Chasma.

Made by: the largest fully covered square (1008 px = 2.0 m) of the mean of R, G, B; divided by its median over 4 cm
cells (median filter, 21 px at half resolution), which keeps grains and pebbles under about 8 cm and cancels the
brightness steps where IDC frames were stitched; made tileable by blending with a half-period shifted copy near the
edges; stored as (ratio - 0.5) * 255, 1024 x 1024. Ratio p1 0.58, median 1.00, p99 1.22, std 0.124.
